# Gift Vouchers

## Introduction

Gift vouchers are always popular around Christmas and other seasonal events- they are a 'go to' option for many if you don't know exactly what the recipient would like.  They can also make very thoughtful gifts for those who are perhaps struggling with household budgets.

You may well find not only that friends and family members of regular and loyal customers of your food enterprise will want to purchase them your gift vouchers for special events, but these customers themselves may want to purchase them and give to their friends (whereby introducing new people to your shop).

## Process

This is a two step process:

* (Optional) [Offer gift vouchers for sale ](how-to-create-gift-vouchers-1.md#step-one)on the OFN platform through your shop front.&#x20;
* After a gift has been purchased, confirm the recipient and apply the gift voucher as credit.

### Step One

Create a [product listing](../../basic-features/products-1/products.md) for vouchers. We suggest that you keep it simple ie. having a maximum of three different denominations of voucher only (£10, £25, £50).

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/gift voucher 2.jpg>)

Remember:

* To include instructions to the customer about how gift vouchers work. You will collect the email address of the customer during the order process automatically, but not the gift recipient - so we advise adding in the product description that customers should enter the recipient's email in the 'Notes' section at checkout.&#x20;
* To keep an eye on orders you receive for your gift vouchers. You can do this manually from order confirmation emails, or use the [Order Cycle Customer Totals report](../../basic-features/reports/order-reports.md#order-cycle-customer-totals) filtered to your gift voucher products.

{% hint style="success" %}
It's a good idea to create a digital voucher image which you can email to the recipient
{% endhint %}

### Step Two

* Locate the gift recipient's email address. If the customer did not already add this into the Notes field at checkout, you'll need to email them to ask for their intended recipient's email address.
* If the recipient is not an existing customer, use the **+ New Customer** button to add the recipient to your **Customers** list using the email address provided by the buyer. The recipient does not need an OFN account first.



<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-27 at 16.19.21.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



* Click the amount in the **Available Credit** column next to the recipient, then the [**Add Credit**](../../basic-features/customers.md#manually-adding-credit) button to award the required amount of credit.

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-27 at 14.37.53.png" alt="" width="323"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-27 at 14.36.35 (1).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>



* In the description box, it's useful to state who the sender was for record keeping:&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-27 at 15.09.48.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



* Send an email to the recipient letting them know they've been sent a gift voucher and the amount (this is where having a digital voucher image to add to the email is useful). Explain that to use the voucher all they need to do is create an OFN account with the same email, and their gift voucher credit will then be automatically available for use at checkout on their next order.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Credit does not expire, and does not need to be used in one order - any remaining balance will be available for use on subsequent orders.
{% endhint %}

## The Recipient's Perspective

The recipient can check their credit balance by logging into OFN, going to their account and clicking the **Customer Transactions** tab. They can view their credit transactions and available credit here:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-10-27 at 16.26.35.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
[Voucher codes](../../basic-features/shopfront/vouchers.md) can also be used for money off orders, but these can be used more than once, need to be deactivated and offer a fixed discount rather than an amount of credit. Voucher codes are therefore best used for short term promotions with a fixed end date.
{% endhint %}
